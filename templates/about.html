About AgroScan

AgroScan is a Final Year Project designed to assist farmers and agricultural experts in identifying plant diseases instantly. Using advanced Deep Learning models, we aim to reduce crop loss and improve food security.

Our platform recognizes widely known plant diseases from simple leaf images, providing accurate diagnostics, causes, and treatment recommendations to ensure healthy harvests.
